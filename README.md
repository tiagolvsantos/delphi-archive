# delphi-archive

Central archive for investment research, quantitative studies, and market analysis supporting the Cerberus ecosystem. Contains stock reports and strategy research produced alongside Cerberus Data and Workbench.

## Contents

### `stock_reports/`

Individual equity research reports in PDF format, organized by ticker symbol. Currently covers **763 tickers** across all major sectors and market caps, including large-cap US equities, international ADRs, crypto-adjacent equities, biotech, shipping, cannabis, and more.

Reports are named `report_<TICKER>_<TIMESTAMP>.pdf` and are generated by the Cerberus Workbench pipeline.
